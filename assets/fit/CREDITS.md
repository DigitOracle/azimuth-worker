## Licence check (4 Oct 2026, https://www.pexels.com/license/)
Allowed: free use on websites and apps, modification and cropping, no attribution required (credit is appreciated, so the page says "Photos: Pexels").
Not allowed: selling unaltered copies, redistributing on other stock/wallpaper platforms, using a photo as a trademark, implying endorsement by the people shown,
and "identifiable people may not appear in a bad light". hero, walk and win show identifiable people (a runner, a walker, hands): THEY MUST ONLY EVER APPEAR WITH POSITIVE
MOMENTS (the header, the progress card, a hit target). Never put one beside a missed-day verdict or any critical message.
